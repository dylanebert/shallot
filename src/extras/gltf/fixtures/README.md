# Fixtures

`normal-uv1.gltf` is a synthetic triangle authored for this package, MIT (see the root `LICENSE`). Its distinct UV_0 and UV_1 sets and `normalTexture.texCoord: 1` exercise the *Using Primitive Data* requirement to generate tangents using the normal texture's coordinates.

`SparseAccessorWithoutBufferView` derives from glTF-Asset-Generator's positive `Output/Positive/Accessor_SparseType/Accessor_SparseType_06.gltf` and `.bin` at [`3d99767e9a67fbfe109f0d298c1e8d909bcac9db`](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Asset-Generator/tree/3d99767e9a67fbfe109f0d298c1e8d909bcac9db/Output/Positive/Accessor_SparseType), under its included MIT `LICENSE`. The quad's positions are translated by `(0.5, 0.5, 0)`; vertex 1 is left zero, and sparse replacements provide vertices 0, 2 and 3. The triangle order is unchanged; indices are narrowed to uint16, and texture, material and animation data are removed. Its POSITION accessor omits `bufferView`: *Accessors* requires zero initialization and permits sparse overrides.

Model directories are unchanged files from [KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/2bac6f8c57bf471df0d2a1e8a8ec023c7801dddf/Models) at `2bac6f8c`, each with its `LICENSE.md`. `default-sampler.gltf` derives from BoxTextured under its license; `required-draco.gltf` and `required-meshopt.gltf` are this package's, MIT. `double-sided.gltf` is a minimal synthetic pair of triangles authored for this package, MIT (see the root `LICENSE`); one material is double-sided and the other omits the flag to exercise its false default.
